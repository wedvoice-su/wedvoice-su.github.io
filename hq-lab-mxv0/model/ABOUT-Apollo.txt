# Apollo (ONNX, for the browser)

ONNX conversion of **Apollo: Band-sequence Modeling for High-Quality Music Restoration in Compressed Audio**
by **Kai Li and Yi Luo** (2024). It restores music damaged by lossy compression (MP3 etc.).

## What was changed (no retraining; the weights are the original ones)

- Only the neural network is in the file. STFT / iSTFT (44.1 kHz, window 882, hop 441, Hann) are done outside, in JavaScript.
- 1×1 convolutions are written as MatMul and the 79 narrow bands are computed together, so it runs with onnxruntime-web (WebGPU or WASM).
- Streaming form: each 7-tap time convolution takes the previous 6 frames as a `state` input, so long songs can be fed in small pieces
  and the result equals whole-file inference. Output is delayed by 54 frames.
- Weights are stored as float16 and cast to float32 when loaded (computation stays float32).

Inputs: `spec` float32 `[1, 2, 442, T]` (real, imaginary; one audio channel), `state` float32 `[18, 80, 6, 256]` (zeros at the start).
Outputs: `out` float32 `[1, 2, 442, T]` (54 frames late), `state_out` float32 `[18, 80, 6, 256]`.

Checked against the original PyTorch computation on the authors' sample: SNR 72.5 dB (float32 version: identical in the interior).
